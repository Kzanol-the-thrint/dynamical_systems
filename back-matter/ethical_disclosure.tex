\subsection*{Methodological Disclosure \& Peer Review Invitation}
\small
\noindent
This paper adopts an interdisciplinary, systems-oriented diagnostic methodology---drawing upon the author's multi-decade foundation across natural science, corporate auditing, systems architecture, and strategic analysis---to expose hidden structural assumptions within foundational paradigms. The conceptual frameworks, hypotheses, and analytical direction were formulated under strict human control through an iterative, collaborative research and drafting process with Google Gemini AI, which served as an analytical and technical writing assistant. While all thematic logic and formalisms have been rigorously cross-examined for internal coherence, the underlying formal models have not been third-party audited; thus, in the spirit of open science and academic transparency, readers and specialists are warmly invited to scrutinize, validate, or offer constructive critiques of the arguments presented herein.
